# NativeBooking Visual Rules

The current website should NOT feel like:

- SaaS template
- AI landing page
- startup pitch deck


Avoid:

- gradient backgrounds
- floating glass cards
- excessive rounded containers
- identical card sections
- generic illustrations
- stock AI imagery


Prefer:

- photography
- real product screenshots
- editorial layouts
- fewer sections
- stronger typography
- asymmetrical layouts


Hero section:

Must answer within 5 seconds:

1. What is NativeBooking?
2. Who is it for?
3. Why should I trust it?
4. What should I do next?


Every section must justify its existence.
