# NativeBooking Brand

## Product

NativeBooking helps property owners and managers handle direct bookings.

## Users

Primary users:

- property owners
- hosts
- property managers
- guests


## Brand personality

NativeBooking should feel:

- trustworthy
- calm
- premium
- professional
- easy


Users should think:

"I can trust this with my business."

"This saves me time."

"This feels reliable."


## We are NOT

Avoid feeling like:

- an AI startup
- crypto product
- developer tool
- cheap booking widget


## Communication style

Use:

- clear language
- confidence
- simplicity
- helpful explanations

Avoid:

- revolutionary
- game-changing
- next generation
- disruptive
- AI-powered everything


## Design emotion

The product should communicate:

Trust > excitement

Clarity > complexity

Professional > flashy
