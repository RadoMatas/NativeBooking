# 1. Discovery Call Script & Qualification

**Goal:** Diagnose pain points, establish authority, demonstrate matching blueprint live, and qualify budget.

---

## Call Outline (15–20 Mins)

### 1. Opening & Rapport (2 Mins)
> *"Thanks for taking the time today, {{First_Name}}. The goal of this call is simple: I want to understand how your business handles appointments and crew dispatches right now, show you how NativeBooking works, and see if a custom scheduling engine makes sense for your team."*

---

### 2. Core Discovery Questions (5 Mins)
1. *"What software are you currently using to take bookings or dispatch staff?"*
2. *"Where is the biggest operational headache right now—is it double-bookings, high no-shows, ugly checkout UI, or manual staff scheduling?"*
3. *"Do off-the-shelf tools feel like they limit how you present your brand or manage client data?"*

---

### 3. Positioning NativeBooking vs. Off-The-Shelf Tools (3 Mins)
> *"Standard widgets like Calendly or Acuity force your business into their generic mold and run on their domains. NativeBooking is custom built for your business. We deploy a dedicated booking engine under your domain that handles custom payment flows, contractor dispatches, and white-label branding."*

---

### 4. Handling Objections

#### Objection: *"Why not just stick with standard tools?"*
* **Response:** *"If standard hourly slots without custom dispatch rules or deposit logic work for you, off-the-shelf tools are fine. But if you need customized intake questions, automated staff dispatching, custom branding on your domain, or tailored deposit logic, generic widgets fall short."*

#### Objection: *"How fast can we go live?"*
* **Response:** *"Our onboarding process takes 7 days from contract signing to live deployment on your custom domain."*

---

### 5. Transition to Closing
> *"Based on what you've shared, our [Tier 1 Essential / Tier 2 Growth] setup fits your exact setup. I can send over our custom proposal and service agreement today. If everything looks good, we can kick off this week. Does that sound good?"*
